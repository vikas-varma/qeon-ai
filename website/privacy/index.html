Privacy Policy

Effective: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Qeon AI ("Qeon AI," "we," "us," or "our") accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our website, mobile application, personal-assistant features, and connected services.

Information we collect

Account information

We may collect your name, email address, authentication identifier, account settings, and information you provide when contacting support.

Google and Gmail information

If you choose to connect Gmail, Google provides authorization tokens and your connected email address. Depending on the permissions you approve, Qeon AI may access message content, headers, senders, recipients, timestamps, labels, threads, attachments you select, and drafts. Qeon AI does not receive or store your Google password.

Usage and device information

We may process app interactions, feature usage, diagnostics, device type, IP address, and security logs to operate, secure, and improve the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users; provide inbox search, summaries, priority detection, task extraction, briefings, and reply drafting; perform actions specifically requested by users; maintain security; troubleshoot problems; and comply with legal obligations.

Google API data and Limited Use

Qeon AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We use Google user data only to provide or improve prominent user-facing Qeon AI features. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, determine creditworthiness, or use it to train or improve generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. Humans do not read Gmail content except with the user's explicit consent for specific support, when required for security, or when legally required.

AI processing

When a user requests an AI-powered email feature, the minimum content needed to fulfill that request may be processed by an AI service provider acting on our instructions. We do not permit providers to use that Gmail data to train generalized models. Before production launch, Qeon AI will identify all subprocessors used for this processing and provide any additional disclosures required by law.

Sharing

We may share information with infrastructure, authentication, security, and AI-processing providers solely as needed to operate user-facing features, under appropriate contractual and security controls. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service. We do not sell personal information.

Storage, security, and retention

We use encryption in transit and protect stored authorization credentials with encryption. We retain account information while an account is active and retain Google-derived information only as long as necessary to provide the requested feature, meet security requirements, or comply with law. Production retention periods and subprocessor details will be published before public launch.

Your controls

You can disconnect Gmail from Qeon AI at any time. Disconnecting revokes or deletes the stored authorization credential. You can also revoke Qeon AI access in your Google Account security settings. You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your Qeon AI account data by contacting us.

Deletion requests

Email vikasjayvee@gmail.com with the subject "Qeon AI data deletion." We will verify the request, disconnect linked accounts, and delete eligible account data, subject to limited legal and security retention obligations.

Children

Qeon AI is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in the user's jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes affecting connected-service data will be presented before the changed use begins where consent is required.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to vikasjayvee@gmail.com.
